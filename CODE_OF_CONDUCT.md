# Community Code of Conduct

Hermex welcomes respectful and collaborative contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differences.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Keep feedback focused on code and ideas.
- No harassment, hate speech, or abuse.

## Not acceptable behavior

- Personal attacks or threats
- Discriminatory or abusive language
- Repeated spam or low-quality disruptive discussion

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- warning,
- temporary community restrictions,
- long-term removal from collaboration in repeated cases.
